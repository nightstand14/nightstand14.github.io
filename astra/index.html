Astra Life OS

Astra is a separate, owner-controlled personal-life application. It brings daily routines, reflections, personal planning and selected task and calendar information into one private workspace.

Why Astra requests Google Calendar access

Astra may read the owner's selected work calendar to display upcoming events and detect scheduling conflicts. When the owner explicitly reviews and approves an event proposal, Astra can use the authorised calendar connection to create that event. Astra does not need access to the owner's unrelated personal calendar to deliver this feature.

Other personal records are managed separately within Astra's own secured application storage. Google Calendar is the source of truth for calendar events, and Notion is used for configured tasks.

Separate from Lila Assistant

Astra and Lila are distinct assistants with their own configurations and integration clients. A handoff from Astra to Lila requires the owner's review; it does not automatically disclose a private journal or send WhatsApp messages.
