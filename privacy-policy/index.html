Privacy Policy

Lila Assistant and Astra Life OS are separate, owner-controlled personal productivity applications. This notice explains how their Google integrations and related application services handle information.

Scope: This policy covers Lila Assistant and Astra Life OS, not the unrelated commercial website of Absolute Optimized. These assistants are not publicly available services, even though this notice is public.

1. Who operates these applications

These applications are administered for the account owner's personal and work-productivity use. Privacy requests and support: marypereira.work@gmail.com. The Google Cloud project may be identified as “Lila Assistant”; Astra Life OS is a separate application with its own Google OAuth client.

2. Information and Google permissions

Only the information covered by permissions the account owner grants through Google OAuth and by features the owner chooses to use is accessed:

  • Google Calendar: Calendar identifiers, event names, dates, times and relevant event details are retrieved for schedule viewing, planning and conflict detection. Where calendar-editing access has been granted, a user-reviewed action may create or update events in the authorised calendar.
  • Google Sheets: For Lila workflows where a Sheets integration is separately enabled and authorised, the assistant may read or update selected spreadsheets to support reports, tracking and tasks requested by the owner. Lila does not obtain unrestricted access merely because a different Google permission has been granted.
  • Account and connection information: OAuth client identifiers, access or refresh tokens, account identifiers, selected calendar identifiers, connection status and technical error logs may be processed to operate, authenticate and troubleshoot integrations. Passwords are not requested by these websites.
  • Other app data: Data supplied to Lila or Astra (such as conversations, tasks, routines and journal entries) is handled within the respective application and its configured service providers; these public information pages do not collect such data.

Google Drive or additional Google services are not included by default. Before expanding the Google data categories or usage, the operator must update disclosures and request the appropriate consent.

3. How information is used

Google information is used only to perform the owner's requested productivity features: display and summarise schedules, plan work, detect calendar conflicts, and perform expressly authorised updates through configured workflows. Selected context may be sent to an enabled AI service to respond to a question or generate a requested summary. Private journal information is not automatically transferred from Astra to Lila.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, targeted ads, data brokerage or credit assessment, and is not made available to unrelated users.

4. Where information is stored and processed

OAuth credentials are intended to be kept in non-public server-side configuration, not published to GitHub Pages. Astra uses hosted application infrastructure and a protected application database for owner-scoped records and time-bounded synced calendar snapshots. Lila uses its separately configured assistant hosting and any authorised connected tools. Service providers—including hosting providers, Google and AI-processing providers when activated—may process the limited information necessary to deliver a feature. Data handling by those providers is subject to their own service terms and technical safeguards.

These public informational pages are statically hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive standard web-request information under its own policies. These pages have no sign-in form, tracking script or contact form.

5. Sharing and transfers

Information is shared only as required to perform an enabled service and the owner's requested operation, such as submitting a calendar change to Google or necessary context to an AI provider. An assistant-to-assistant handoff requires user review. No information is sold to advertisers or data brokers. Providers may process information in other countries according to their infrastructure and agreements.

6. Security and access controls

The applications are intended for authorised owner access, with application authentication, server-side credentials and limited third-party permissions. Where supported, significant external changes require explicit review and approval. No internet-connected service can guarantee perfect security. Public GitHub Pages files contain no Google OAuth tokens, passwords or private account data.

7. Retention and deletion

Calendar snapshots, operational records and app content may be retained as needed for the configured service, backups and troubleshooting; durations vary by data type and hosting provider. Astra's temporary assistant chat history is intended to be retained for 30 days under its app policy, while some user-saved records persist until deleted. Disconnecting Google access prevents future API requests but does not automatically remove information previously stored in app records or backups. The owner can request inspection or deletion of app-held personal data using the contact email above, subject to technical and legal retention constraints.

8. Your controls

  • Review and revoke Google access via Google Account connections.
  • Manage individual service permissions through the corresponding Google and application settings.
  • Request access to, correction of or deletion of applicable stored app information by emailing marypereira.work@gmail.com.
  • Where an application provides an export or delete feature, use that feature for the records it manages.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the applications' integrations, data types or handling practices materially change. An updated effective date will be posted here. Newly requested Google permissions require the appropriate consent before they are used.

10. Contact

Questions about this notice or data handling: marypereira.work@gmail.com.
