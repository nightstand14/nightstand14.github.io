Lila Assistant

A private assistant for everyday operations.

Lila Assistant is an owner-controlled digital assistant that helps organise work, calendars, tasks and connected productivity tools. The assistant is configured for authorised personal use, not offered as an open public service.

Lila Assistant

Helps the account owner review schedules, plan work and handle authorised productivity workflows, including Google Calendar and, where connected, Google Sheets.

Data access follows the permissions approved by the account owner. Changes to external resources are subject to the enabled workflow and user controls.

Astra Life OS

A separate personal-life application for daily planning, reflections, routines and calendar visibility. It uses its own OAuth client for the work calendar.

Astra and Lila are distinct assistants; they do not share a public login or automatically exchange private information.

Google account access and privacy

These applications request Google permissions only through the owner's explicit OAuth authorisation. Connected Google data is used to provide requested calendar or productivity features, not for advertising or sale.

For the categories of data used, storage, approved service providers, revocation and deletion requests, see the privacy policy.
